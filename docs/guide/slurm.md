# Slurm

Running, waiting and finished jobs in three sections that collapse and resize like the ones in Files: live `squeue`, and recent `sacct` history with exit codes and memory, where jobs that failed, timed out or ran out of memory are marked in red. A fourth section at the bottom shows what the cluster has free and in use (CPUs, memory, GPUs and the jobs waiting, for the whole cluster and per partition). Click a job for its log viewer, which follows `stdout`/`stderr`; a job that hasn't finished opens on its details instead. The *This project / All jobs* switch in the header chooses between the project's jobs (the default) and all your jobs. A job counts as the project's if its Slurm comment is the project's tag, `codeome:<project>-<id>`, which Codeome adds with `sbatch --comment` when you submit through it, or if it ran inside one of the project's saved HPC folders (see [Project settings](/guide/project-settings#hpc-folders)). Jobs started outside Codeome, such as ehio or Drakkar batches, appear under *This project* only when their working folder is inside a saved folder. With no folders saved, the folder open in the HPC Files tab is used, and while that is `~` every job is shown. Also cancel, submit (`sbatch`) from the editor, and a job-script template.
